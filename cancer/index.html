Cancer Research — Accessibility Series

As a speaker, I organized and delivered a month-long series of presentations to propagate cancer awareness and make cancer education more accessible to youth. By simplifying complex scientific concepts into engaging, easy-to-understand discussions, I sought to bridge the gap between advanced cancer research and public understanding. Through these sessions, I aimed not only to educate young people about cancer, its prevention, early detection, and emerging treatments, but also to inspire them to propagate this knowledge within their own communities, creating a ripple effect of awareness and scientific curiosity.
